WELCOME TO THE TOWER

Your airspace.
Your call.

Select an aircraft in the sky or the traffic list. Assign 27R or 27L to clear an arrival to land or a departure to take off. Each safe landing or departure earns 100 points.

  1. Continuous arrivals and departures. Switch traffic tabs. Clear arrivals to land. For departures: approve pushback, issue taxi clearance, accept the ground-to-tower handover, then clear takeoff when the runway and final are clear. Hand climbing flights to London Departure.
  2. Draw your own vectors. Click Draw route, then drag a path in the overhead view. Assign a runway afterwards to complete the approach.
  3. Mistakes have consequences. Collisions end your shift immediately. Runway conflicts, separation losses, fuel diversions and excessive departure delays count as strikes; three strikes end the shift. Keep them separated. Red alerts mean aircraft are within the game's compressed 450 m horizontal / 90 m vertical limit. Three losses end your shift.
  4. Hold outside the airport. Hold sends aircraft to an external racetrack stack. Radar shows all four stacks. Go around cancels an approach and climbs away.
1–4 cameras · Space pause · H hide HUD · F flythrough · M sound · Esc exit

A stylised game with reference-informed airport geometry. Traffic, weather and procedures are simulated; distances and time are compressed for play. Not affiliated with Heathrow, NATS or any airline.

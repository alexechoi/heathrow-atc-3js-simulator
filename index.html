WELCOME TO THE TOWER

Your airspace.
Your call.

Select an aircraft in the sky or the traffic list. Assign 27R or 27L to clear it onto a stabilised approach.

  1. Sequence arrivals. Leave room between aircraft on the same final. Use Hold to delay an arrival.
  2. Draw your own vectors. Click Draw route, then drag a path in the overhead view. Assign a runway afterwards to complete the approach.
  3. Keep them separated. Red alerts mean aircraft are within the game's compressed 450 m horizontal / 90 m vertical limit. Three losses end your shift.
  4. Go around. Cancel an approach and climb away when things get crowded.
1–4 cameras · Space pause · H hide HUD · F flythrough · M sound · Esc exit

A stylised game with reference-informed airport geometry. Traffic, weather and procedures are simulated; distances and time are compressed for play. Not affiliated with Heathrow, NATS or any airline.
